02

AGENT 会THE AGENT读完资料，提出核心命题、章节顺序，以及每章要证明什么。reads the material and proposes a thesis, the chapter order, and what each chapter must prove.
你来做YOU调整命题和顺序。这一步改起来最便宜，越往后改越贵。reshape the thesis and the order. Changes are cheapest here and get dearer later.

03需要你确认YOU CONFIRM

母稿：逐章修改，逐章确认Manuscript: edit and confirm chapter by chapter

AGENT 会THE AGENT整章写出每页的核心内容、上屏文字、讲解方向，在浏览器里打开工作台。writes whole chapters — each page’s point, on-screen copy and talk track — and opens the studio in your browser.
你来做YOU选中文字直接评论，或者直接改；切到“线框”看每页大概的版面。select any text to comment or edit it in place; switch to “Wireframe” to see each page’s rough layout.
你拍板YOU DECIDE每页或每章点“确认”。确认后如果再改，会自动退回待确认。confirm page by page or a whole chapter. Edit it afterwards and it drops back to unconfirmed automatically.
