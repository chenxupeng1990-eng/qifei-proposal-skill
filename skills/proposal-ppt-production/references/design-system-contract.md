# Project DESIGN.md contract

Before visual production, the project must provide a confirmed `DESIGN.md`. This reference defines the minimum contract; it does not prescribe a client style.

## Required sections

1. **Canvas and safe area** — aspect ratio, render size, content margins, footer behavior, and whether page numbers are shown.
2. **Brand assets** — approved logo, packaging, product photography, fonts, source locations, and prohibited alterations. Separate corporate-brand assets from product/category assets; a product image alone is not a complete brand input.
3. **Color tokens** — primary, secondary, accent, light/dark surfaces, text colors, contrast rules, and permitted gradients. Define which colors establish brand identity and which colors establish product/category mood; do not automatically promote product colors into the corporate brand palette.
4. **Typography bands** — cover, chapter, content title, module title, body, annotation, and source sizes. Same-role sizes must remain consistent across pages. Unless a confirmed template overrides them, the minimum defaults are 50pt deck title, 35pt slide title, 24pt mid-level heading and 16pt body. Define title line intent and prohibit silent wrapping of intended single-line titles.
5. **Page families** — cover, contents, chapter, judgment, evidence, method, scene, operating, and closing pages. Define hierarchy, not a single repeated layout.
6. **Visual hammer** — the recurring semantic form that carries the proposal's central idea. State where it may appear and how it must not compete with the page conclusion.
7. **Carrier rules** — when to use tables, charts, timelines, processes, scenes, UI/work artifacts, transparent semantic elements, or a full visual composition.
8. **Image generation rules** — reference images, product fidelity, lighting/material language, allowed no-text icons, transparent PNG requirements, and banned generated text or unverifiable labels.
9. **HTML precision layer** — which copy, data, labels, sources, and annotations must remain editable and exact.
10. **Whitespace and focus** — target occupancy bands, maximum unexplained continuous whitespace, first visual focus, and orphan-element checks.
11. **Review states** — draft, in review, approved, assembly-ready, and superseded. Only explicit owner approval advances a page.
12. **Logic-to-visual QA** — require a causal statement, carrier removal test, carrier swap test, thumbnail test, and product role before visual production.
13. **Visual-frame contract** — aspect ratio, subject placement, gaze/direction, protected copy area, crop tolerance and image-reuse rule for every generated or sourced hero visual.
14. **Deck-level visual authority** — exact source template, approved custom direction or newly calibrated direction. Only one may control the deck.
15. **Brand-presence level** — `light`, `medium`, or `strong`, with the minimum official brand anchors, brand-DNA visual grammar, page locations, and product/category protections. `light` remains recognizable without turning every page into a corporate brand campaign.

## Authority order

`confirmed source content` → `page contract` → `project DESIGN.md` → `this generic production reference`

If the project has no confirmed `DESIGN.md`, stop after producing a small calibration set. Do not scale a guessed visual language across the deck.

## Non-negotiable separation

- Image generation may own scenes, materials, spatial relationships, people, and no-text semantic icons.
- Up to three short exact semantic labels may be integrated into Image2 only when they are inseparable from one visual hammer and verified character by character.
- HTML owns exact words, numbers, units, sources, dense logic, and unverifiable labels.
- Review uses the artifact required by the selected production route: HTML plus independent PNG renders for editable information pages, or direct PNG plus Cowart annotations for image-native pages.
- PPTX/PDF assembly begins only after all required pages are explicitly approved.
